# Design analysis (Saily)

- **Palette:** white canvas, near-black text, single saturated yellow for primary CTA and search button, soft grey panels, small coloured badges (cashback, savings in green, sale price in red).
- **Type:** geometric sans throughout, large bold H1 (~40 px desktop), compact body.
- **Layout:** 2-column destination hero (photo left 40 %, purchase right 60 %), 3-column plan cards on desktop, single-column rows on mobile with price right-aligned.
- **Components:** radio plan cards, "Best Choice" black ribbon, stepper for days, sticky checkout, segmented tabs, accordion FAQ, comparison table with check marks, review carousel, mega menus (Product/Resources/Offers/Help), language + currency dropdowns.
- **Motion:** minimal; overlay fade, tab switches. No 3D.
- **Mobile:** hamburger nav, full-width CTA, plan rows tappable full width.

Takeaways for BLEUSIM: keep commerce screens just as calm and legible (Universe B), but own the brand through the dark cinematic globe (Universe A), BLEUSIM blue as CTA colour instead of yellow, and an original type pairing.
